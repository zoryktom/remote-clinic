# Experimental Protocol

## First Experiment

Compare:

- Scenario A: offline, no AI, two staff, constrained supplies
- Scenario B: offline, local structured AI, two staff, constrained supplies

Use:

- population: 50
- duration: 30 simulated days
- runs: 5
- matched seed family
